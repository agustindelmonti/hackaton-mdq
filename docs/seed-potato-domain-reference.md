## 0. Papasud in the public record — read this first

**[F]** Papasud S.A. is not a generic potato grower: in the only academic study of the sector it is **the largest seed-potato exporter in Argentina, with 82.2% of the country's total exported seed-potato volume over 2013–2023**, exporting continuously since 2003. Its declared destinations: **Brasil, Paraguay, Uruguay, Guatemala, República Dominicana, Venezuela, Vietnam, Angola**. For 2024 it reported export commitments of **1,791 t** against an exportable net production of **4,469.16 t** (i.e. ~2,678 t of exportable surplus). Its stated competitive edge: *"Holanda y Canadá exportan semillas de 4ta a 7ma generación, desde Argentina Papasud exporta 3ra generación"* — younger seed, higher sanitary status. Its stated pain points: market access not opened by SENASA (Egypt, Indonesia, Malaysia), **Brazil and Uruguay requiring washed seed** (extra cost + disease risk), and non-equivalent category tolerances between Argentina and Brazil. Source: D'Alessandro, Mariela — *Evaluación del modelo agroexportador de papa semilla en Argentina*, Trabajo final de graduación, Ingeniería Agronómica, Pontificia Universidad Católica Argentina (undated, data through 2024). Based on the INASE management system (not public), SENASA export records, and direct interviews with the country's two main exporters, **Drakar S.R.L.** and **Papasud S.A.** — *I read the full PDF but could not pin its canonical public URL; search for the exact title to re-locate it. Treat the figures as sourced-but-single-source.*

**Terminology signal that will land in the room:** say *"papa semilla fiscalizada"*, *"semillero"*, *"lote"*, *"subcategoría"*, *"campaña 25/26"*, *"zona diferenciada"*, *"rótulo"*, *"generación"*. Do **not** say "seed batch", "seed lot number" in English, or "certified organic".

## 1. Production cycle — Sudeste Bonaerense (Balcarce / Mar del Plata / Tandil / Tres Arroyos)

### 1.1 Campaign convention
**[F]** Campaigns are named **split-year**, e.g. `2025/2026` or `25/26`, because planting is in year 1 (Oct–Nov) and harvest/marketing in year 2 (Feb–Jun). Both INASE reports and the trade press use this ([INASE informe papa 2019/2020](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/inase_informe_papa2020.pdf); [La Nueva, campaña 2025/2026](http://www.lanueva.com/nota/2026-4-21-5-0-0-papa-el-sudeste-bonaerense-avanza-hacia-la-logica-de-producir-menos-para-ganar-mas)).

**One cycle per year** in the sudeste. Only Córdoba runs two (semitemprana July–Aug + tardía February). Tucumán/NOA is the *temprana* window. **[F]**

### 1.2 Calendar (sudeste bonaerense = "semitardía")

| Stage | Months | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Preparación de suelo | May–Jun (autumn) | 2 passes rastra de discos pesada, 2 passes subsolador 40–50 cm, then rastra liviana / rolo desterronador **[F]** ([El Diario de Tandil](https://www.eldiariodetandil.com/nota-papa-paso-a-paso-para-una-correcta-plantacion-y-cosecha-82960)) |

| **Plantación** | **Oct–Nov** | **>96% of Buenos Aires area is planted Oct–Nov** **[F]** ([Guía didáctica cultivo y manejo de la papa, FCAyF-UNLP, 2020](https://aulavirtual.agro.unlp.edu.ar/pluginfile.php/60751/mod_folder/content/0/Gu%C3%ADa%20papa%202020.pdf)) |

| Clasificación / envasado / rotulado | Apr–Sep | |

Crop cycle length: short-cycle cultivars 90–100 d, intermediate 110–120 d, long 130–140 d. **[F]**

### 1.3 Multiplication scheme — GET THIS RIGHT

**[F]** Current framework: **Resolución INASE 171/2000** (repealing Res. 63/2000, which had itself inserted *Prefundación* into Res. INASE 245/98), plus **Res. 217/02** and **Res. INASE 135/2022**. Sources: [Res. 171/2000 full text (InfoLeg)](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/60000-64999/64565/norma.htm), [Res. 171/2000 (argentina.gob.ar)](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-64565/texto), [Res. 135/2022](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/resoluci%C3%B3n-135-2022-376199/texto).

Two **classes/categorías**, each with ordered **subcategorías**:

```
CLASE: FISCALIZADA
├── Categoría BÁSICA
│   ├── (condiciones controladas — laboratorio / invernáculo)
│   │   ├── Preinicial 0      ← in vitro: microplantas, microtubérculos (ex meristema apical)
│   │   ├── Preinicial I      ← invernáculo (minitubérculos)
│   │   └── Preinicial II     ← invernáculo
│   └── (producida a campo)
│       ├── Inicial I         ← 1st field generation, from Preinicial II or better
│       ├── Inicial II
│       ├── Inicial III
│       ├── Prefundación      ← inserted by Res. 63/2000; same requirements as Inicial III
│       └── Fundación
└── Categoría CERTIFICADA
    ├── Registrada
    └── Certificada           ← terminal: "su único destino es originar cultivos para consumo"
```

**Mapping to G0/G1/G2…** — **[E]** with **[F]** anchors. Argentina does **not** officially label categories G0/G1/G2; the working mapping used in practice is:

| Generation | Argentine subcategoría | Where produced |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | Preinicial 0 | in vitro |
| G0/G1 | Preinicial I, Preinicial II | invernáculo, minitubérculos |
| G1 | Inicial I | 1st year in field |
| G2 | Inicial II | 2nd year in field |
| G3 | Inicial III / Prefundación | 3rd year in field |
| G4 | Fundación | |
| G5+ | Registrada, Certificada | |

**[F] CRITICAL DATA-MODEL INSIGHT — category ≠ generation counter.** Under **Res. 217/02** the category a lot obtains depends on **both** the sanitary results **and how many samples the semillero chose to have analysed per lot**: *"cuando se toma una sola muestra la categoría desciende hasta REGISTRADA y cuando se toman 3 muestras la categoría desciende a FUNDACIÓN"*. INASE's own 2019/20 report states that most Buenos Aires production landed in **Fundación** and **Registrada** *not* for sanitary reasons but because of sampling choices — on sanitary grounds alone most would have been **Inicial II / Inicial III** ([INASE informe papa 2020](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/inase_informe_papa2020.pdf)).
→ **Model `generacion` (integer, derived from parent lot) and `categoria_obtenida` (result of fiscalización, per lot per campaign) as two separate fields.** Also model `muestras_tomadas` (int) — it is a commercial decision with a category consequence. This single detail will read as insider knowledge.

**[F]** Export reality: the categories actually traded for export are **REGISTRADA and CERTIFICADA**; Papasud markets "3rd generation". Producing an exportable seed lot takes **3–4 years** from in-vitro.

### 1.4 Zona diferenciada (regulatory geography)
**[F]** Buenos Aires seed-potato **zona diferenciada**: partidos of **Tres Arroyos, Adolfo Gonzáles Chaves, San Cayetano** (Decreto provincial 1673/03; earlier Decreto 5383/90). Inside it, planting is restricted to **Categoría Básica** seed (Inicial III or better in the strictest reading) and **no papa consumo may be produced**. Other national seed zones: **Malargüe (Mendoza)**, **Tafí del Valle (Tucumán)**, **Valle de Las Estancias (Catamarca)**, **Valle Bonaerense del Río Colorado**, San Juan. ~3,000 ha/yr of seed potato in the BA zone across 32–37 semilleros. **[F]**

**[F]** Lot isolation rule (Res. 171/2000 art. 34/10): between two neighbouring lots leave **2 potato-free furrows** for the same subcategoría+cultivar, **3 furrows** for different subcategoría or cultivar. Rotation: **no potato for 3 years prior** (1 year minimum only with an INASE-approved soil management plan). Volunteer potato (*papa espontánea*) breaks the rotation count. Lots must carry a **cartel indicador** at the head of the lot stating **variedad, número de lote, subcategoría**.

### 3.2 Dormancy / sprouting
**[F]** Dormición (dormancia) lasts **2–6 months** depending on cultivar (short 1–1.5 mo, intermediate 2–3.5 mo, long 4–6 mo). Storage at 4–6 °C prolongs it; abrupt 25→10 °C swings shorten it. Physiological stages the tuber passes through, in order: **dormancia → dominancia apical → brotación múltiple (optimal for planting) → senescencia** (tiny tubers straight off the sprouts = "brotación directa", seed no longer usable). Dormancy break agents: tiourea 1%, tiocianato de K 1%, AG (ácido giberélico) 1–10 ppm. Sprout inhibition for consumo: **hidrazida maleica** (8 l/ha of 30% commercial product, 30–40 d before harvest), plus antibrotantes in store — **never on seed lots**. **Desbrote** (removing the apical sprout) is a real work order: it breaks apical dominance and multiplies stems. Sprouts >3 cm should be desbrotado before planting to avoid handling breakage.
**Key rule for a demo:** *sprouting is a defect in consumo and a requirement in semilla.* A seed lot is planted at the **brotación múltiple** stage with short, thick, vigorous sprouts, developed under diffuse light.

### 3.3 Grading / calibres
**[F] What is sourced:** Argentine practice grades seed mostly by **weight/tamaño classes** — *semilla delgada* or *tercera* (small, planted whole), *segunda*, *primera* (**>100 g**). Planting seed target 40–80 g. Fresh market ships in **50 kg** arpillera/polypropylene bags; the Argentine tipificación is "por grados de calidad y clase de tamaño". Bastón industry **rejects tubers under 50 mm**. Industry accepts lots with **dry matter >18%**, optimum **20–23%** (starch >24%).

**[E] mm calibre grid for the prototype** (international seed-potato convention, adapt if a Papasud person corrects you):
`25/35 mm` · `28/35` · `35/45` · `45/55` · `55/65` · `>65 (para trozar)`
Plus quality grades for the demo: `Extra` / `Primera` / `Segunda` / `Descarte`. Defect vocabulary that is real **[F]**: *verdeado, rajadura, corazón hueco, corazón negro, crecimiento secundario, lenticelosis, daño mecánico (mancha grisácea), pelado*.

### 4.2 Inputs — real active ingredients used on potato in Argentina

**[F] sourced as actually used in Argentine potato:**
- **Fungicidas:** **mancozeb** (the single most-used product, ~7-day intervals, usually tank-mixed with an insecticide), **propineb**, **clorotalonil**; plus **estrobilurinas** and **triazoles** for Alternaria. Referenced Argentine studies count 20–23 applications/cycle and up to 40 distinct agrochemicals on the crop. ([CASAFE — Plagas y adversidades en el cultivo de papa](https://www.casafe.org/plagas-adversidades-cultivo-papa/); [Uso de agroquímicos en el cultivo de papa, Dialnet](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/5040034.pdf))
- **Insecticidas:** **imidacloprid**, **abamectina** and **cartap** (specifically for *Liriomyza*), historically **endosulfán** (now banned in Argentina — do not put it in a demo as current). **[F]**
- **Herbicidas:** pre-plant / pre-emergence **metribuzín, linurón, EPTC, alaclor**; **paraquat / diquat** as total herbicide for foliage desiccation. **[F]**
- **Fertilizantes:** **fosfato diamónico (DAP) 18-46-0** banded at planting 2 cm below and beside the seed; **urea 45-0-0** at 250–300 kg/ha (1st aporque) and 350 kg/ha (2nd aporque) — or **sulfato de amonio**; K raises calibre and dry matter. **[F]**
- **Reguladores / otros:** **hidrazida maleica** 8 l/ha of 30% product, 30–40 d pre-harvest (consumo only); **ácido giberélico** 1–10 ppm; **cal hidratada** on cut seed. **[F]**
- **Biológico:** **baculovirus** (powder bioinsecticide) against polilla de la papa in storage. **[F]**

**[E] Commercial brand names** — plausible in Argentina but I did not verify current SENASA registration per product: Ridomil Gold (metalaxil-M + mancozeb), Curzate (cimoxanilo + mancozeb), Bravo (clorotalonil), Acrobat (dimetomorf + mancozeb), Amistar (azoxistrobina), Infinito (propamocarb + fluopicolide), Confidor (imidacloprid), Coragen (clorantraniliprol). **Use active ingredients, not brands, in the demo** — active ingredients are verifiable and brands are where a wrong detail gets caught.

## 5. Pests and diseases — and photo-detectability

**Photo-detectability legend:** **📷 LEAF** = plausibly classifiable from a leaf/canopy photo · **🥔 TUBER** = needs a tuber photo · **🔬 LAB** = requires ELISA/PCR/bioassay, **not** photo-detectable · **🌱 WHOLE PLANT** = needs plant habit/context, hard from a single leaf.

### Fungal / oomycete
| Spanish | Scientific | Detectability | Key visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tizón tardío** (mildew, "papa chocolate" in tuber) | *Phytophthora infestans* | **📷 LEAF — best candidate** | Dark irregular blotches starting at leaflet margin/apex, **grey-white downy growth on the underside**; stems blacken and become glassy; tuber cortex browning. Favoured by cool wet mornings 13–18 °C, dew/fog; halted >30 °C. **[F]** |
| **Tizón temprano** | *Alternaria solani* | **📷 LEAF — good candidate** | Concentric "tiro al blanco" (target-board) lesions on **oldest leaves first**, then defoliation. Favoured by high humidity + 29–30 °C. **[F]** |
| **Sarna negra / rizoctoniasis / costra negra** | *Rhizoctonia solani* (anamorph; *Thanatephorus cucumeris*) | **🥔 TUBER + 🌱 WHOLE PLANT** | Black sclerotia stuck to tuber skin ("costra negra"), net-like russeting; in plant: emergence failure, stem-base cankers, upper-leaf rolling and chlorosis, **aerial tubers**, clustered/cracked/greened tubers. **[F]** |

### Viruses — **all 🔬 LAB, none photo-detectable with credibility**
**[F]** *"La raza YN del PVY es la más frecuente en Argentina y produce mosaicos leves difícilmente detectables a la vista"* — say this out loud in the demo; it is exactly the kind of honesty that earns trust.
- **PVY** (mosaico severo / rugoso) — *Potato virus Y*. BA seed 2019/20: **1.21%** average incidence. **[F]**
- **PLRV** (enrollado de la hoja) — *Potato leafroll virus*. BA 2019/20: **0.04%**. **[F]**
- **PVX** (mosaico latente) — *Potato virus X*. BA 2019/20: **0%**. **[F]**
- PVX+PVY (mosaico rugoso), **mosaico deformante**, **TSWV** (muerte del brote apical). **[F]**
- Detection: **ELISA on microplate readers is mandatory and INASE-regulated** for seed lots; PCR and indicator plants also used. **[F]**
- **[F]** Argentina reduced PVY/PLRV in seed from ~70–80% incidence in the 1990s to near-zero today by banning seed imports and building domestic in-vitro capacity. This is the single proudest fact in the Argentine seed-potato story — reference it.

## 6. Certification and regulatory framework

### 6.1 Who does what
- **INASE** (Instituto Nacional de Semillas) — **seed identity, category and quality**. Runs the **RNCyFS** (Registro Nacional de Comercio y Fiscalización de Semillas); every semillero has an RNCyFS inscription number. Registers lots, inspects fields, supervises harvest/conditioning/packaging/labelling, issues the **rótulo oficial**, runs/approves the ELISA labs, issues the **certificado de importación/exportación de semillas**. Legal base: **Ley de Semillas 20.247/73**; potato standard **Res. INASE 171/2000** (+ 217/02, 135/2022); potato seed export **Res. SAGyP 715/94**; seed import/export procedure **Res. 56/18**. **[F]**
- **SENASA** — **phytosanitary status and border**. Issues the **Certificado Fitosanitario de Exportación** (now **ePhyto** to Brazil since 4 Aug 2023 with MAPA), manages **AFIDI** (Autorización Fitosanitaria de Importación) for inbound, and the **CERT-POV** self-service system. **[F]** ([SENASA export certification](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/servicio/productos-de-origen-vegetal-certificacion-fitosanitaria-de-exportacion); [ePhyto with Brazil](https://www.cda.org.ar/detalle_noticia.php?id=42033))
- **Provincial authorities** — the *zona diferenciada* decrees (Decreto Pcial. Bs.As. 1673/03, 5383/90; Ley Pcial. Mendoza 5326/82 + Res. ISCAMEN 11-I-96; Ley Pcial. Catamarca 4468/85). **[F]**
- **INTA (EEA Balcarce)** — breeding, agronomy, extension. Not a regulator. **[F]**

### 6.2 Field process, in order (each step is a record in your data model) **[F]**
1. **Preinscripción de lotes** — 30 days before planting: cadastral location, access directions, **croquis de ubicación del lote**, **3-year soil/crop history**.
2. **Inscripción definitiva** — before the first inspection: declare the **subcategoría planted**, attach the **rótulo and purchase invoice of the seed used** (showing number of packages, weight, cultivar, category), breeder's authorisation for protected cultivars, **Declaración de Destino Final de la Producción (Anexo XII)** of the previous campaign, each lot presented separately with **dimensions and planting diagram**, fee paid.
3. **Identificación a campo** — cartel at the lot head: **variedad, número de lote, subcategoría**.
4. **Inspecciones de cultivo** — at least **two**: the first 4–6 weeks after emergence, the second shortly before foliage destruction.
5. **Muestreo post-control** — 15 to 30 days after foliage destruction, before harvest. For controlled-condition (Preinicial) lots: 500 units per lot, not exceeding 10% of the lot's plants, of which **400 to the lab** and **100 for varietal purity**.
6. **Análisis de laboratorio** — ELISA for PVY/PVX/PLRV; nematode analysis.
7. **Categorización** — outcome combines sanitary results **and** number of samples (Res. 217/02).
8. **Acondicionamiento, envasado, rotulado**.

### 6.3 What a lot physically carries **[F]**
**Rótulo oficial** (Res. 171/2000 art. 16/40, format per Anexo V of Res. 42/2000), on **new** containers:
- Clase **fiscalizada**
- **Categoría y subcategoría**
- **Variedad (cultivar)**
- **Zona de producción**
- **Año de cosecha**
- Name and **RNCyFS inscription number** of the producer
- **"MATERIAL TRATADO CON VENENO"** in red letters if chemically treated
- Grade applied with ink stamp
- Max **50 kg** (field-produced) / **20 kg** (Preinicial); bins/big bags/granel per Res. 135/2022

**Open containers >50 kg** must additionally show: the legend **"PAPA SEMILLA FISCALIZADA EN PROCESO"**, the multiplier's name/address/inscription number, **the name of the field where it was produced**, **lote de producción**, variedad, weight, and zona de producción. → *This is your traceability spec, handed to you by the regulation.*

### 6.4 Export documentation **[F]**
INASE side (Res. 56/18, via **TAD** platform):
- Import/export request form
- **Nota de compromiso** of non-diffusion for material not in the Catálogo Nacional de Cultivares
- Factura and/or **Packing List**
- **VEP** payment receipt (~AR$5,950/application at time of publication; validity 60 days; 48–96 h processing)
- RNCyFS registration
- Potato-specific: **Res. SAGyP 715/94**

SENASA side (via **CERT-POV**):
- Registration in AFIP's Registro de Operadores de Comercio Exterior
- Export procedure request + **Certificado Fitosanitario** application
- **Permiso de importación / AFIDI** of the destination country
- Copy of the **permiso de embarque** (customs)
- **Copia del Certificado del INASE para material de propagación** ← the seed-specific hook
- Official documentation of the importing NPPO's phytosanitary requirements
- Fee receipt

Plus the standard commercial pack **[F]/[E]**: **factura comercial / proforma**, **packing list**, **certificado de origen MERCOSUR** (issued by a Cámara de Comercio Exterior — the Mercosur form is a real standardised document), **conocimiento de embarque / CRT** for road freight to Brazil/Paraguay.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Brasil** | 9,500 | 2013–2023 (every year except 2015) |
| 2 | **Paraguay** | 4,354 | 2015–2023 |

Market-specific **[F]**:
- **Brasil and Uruguay require washed seed** (*semilla lavada*) — raises cost and disease risk; Canada and the EU do not, which is a competitive disadvantage for Argentina.
- **Brasil's category tolerances differ from Argentina's** (Brazil's are looser), so Argentine *Certificada* may be re-offered in Brazil under a lower category name. No unified criteria. **This is a genuine unsolved business problem — a category-equivalence mapping table would be a credible feature.**
- **AFIDI-mandated analyses are repeated at destination**, holding the buyer's seed 20–30 days before it can be planted.
- **MERCOSUR/GMC/Res. 29/22** sets the equivalences: max lot to be sampled **200 t**, defined sample size for virus determination, lab methods for nematodes, and recognises **visual analysis** for fungi/bacteria/defects; the importer may request an additional quality/pest analysis.
- **ePhyto** is the official channel to Brazil since 2023-08-04.
- Markets requested but **not opened by SENASA**: Egypt (one Egyptian client alone asked for 5,000 t), Indonesia, Malaysia. **[F]**

Entities a demo extractor should pull from these: `labor`, `lote_ref` (short **and** full form), `variedad`, `producto` / `ingrediente_activo`, `dosis` + `unidad`, `superficie` + `unidad`, `plaga_enfermedad`, `ubicacion`, `calibre`, `envase` + `cantidad`, `rendimiento`, `fecha_relativa` ("hoy a la mañana", "el jueves", "a partir del 20"), `actor` (inspector INASE), `estado_fenologico` ("plena tuberización"), `alerta` (temperature deviation).

## 9. Ten facts to drop in the pitch (all **[F]**)

1. Campaigns are `2025/2026`; the sudeste plants **Oct–Nov** and harvests **Feb–Apr** — **one cycle a year**, so a data model with an annual campaign key is correct, not lazy.
2. The Argentine category ladder is **Preinicial 0/I/II → Inicial I/II/III → Prefundación → Fundación → Registrada → Certificada**, split into *Básica* and *Certificada* classes (Res. INASE 171/2000).
3. **Category is not generation.** Res. 217/02 ties the category obtained to the **number of samples the semillero pays to have analysed** (1 → Registrada, 3 → Fundación). Model them separately.
4. Producing an **exportable** seed lot takes **3–4 years** from in vitro. Papasud exports **3rd generation** vs Holland/Canada's 4th–7th — that is the product.
5. Buenos Aires is **79.4%** of all fiscalizada seed potato in Argentina; the **zona diferenciada** is **Tres Arroyos, A. Gonzáles Chaves, San Cayetano**, where *papa consumo may not be grown at all*.
6. Average seed lot ≈ **5.6 ha**; typical seeding rate **2–3 t/ha**; rows **80–90 cm**; **~30,000–45,000 plantas/ha**.
7. Seed potato stores at **4–5 °C / 92–95% HR**; industry fry potatoes at **7–8 °C** because cold builds reducing sugars; **never below 2 °C**; **curado at 18 °C for 10–15 days** on entry.
8. Argentina's seed PVY incidence fell from ~70–80% in the 1990s to **1.21%** (PLRV 0.04%, PVX 0%, nematodes 0%) — achieved by **banning seed imports** and building in-vitro capacity.
9. **PVY/PLRV are invisible to the eye** (race YN gives faint mosaics) — photo AI must not claim them; ELISA is mandatory and INASE-regulated.
10. Export needs **both** an **INASE certificate for propagation material** and a **SENASA certificado fitosanitario (ePhyto to Brazil since Aug 2023)**; **Brazil and Uruguay demand washed seed** and their category tolerances do not map onto Argentina's.
